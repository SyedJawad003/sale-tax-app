BUILD THE APK (free, no installs on your PC)
1. Create a free account at github.com and make a NEW repository (private is fine).
2. Click "uploading an existing file" and drag in ALL files from this zip, including the
   hidden .github folder (or unzip, then drag the whole contents). Commit.
3. Open the repository's Actions tab -> "Build APK" -> wait about 5 minutes for the green tick.
4. Open that run -> Artifacts -> download "invoice-maker-apk" (zip) -> unzip -> app-debug.apk.
5. Send app-debug.apk to your phone, tap it, and allow "Install unknown apps" when asked.
Invoices are saved to Documents and the share sheet opens. Numbering starts at 187 and goes up by one after each saved invoice.
